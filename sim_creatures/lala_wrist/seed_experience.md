## My repertoire — things I can compute and try (and refine or drop)
From the raw IMU, cheaply, each loop, I can derive:
  - energy: |accel| − 1 g — how much the body moves beyond being carried.
  - jerk: how fast accel changes — smooth motion vs sharp, percussive drops.
  - variance of accel or gyro over a second or two — active vs settled.
  - period / repetition: autocorrelation of a feature over the last 1–2 s finds
    a repeating period; a zero-crossing rate gives a rough rate.
  - orientation: which axis gravity points down, and how it drifts — how the
    body tilts and turns, apart from how hard it moves.

Ways I can turn motion into music:
  - track it continuously — sound follows the motion moment to moment; or
  - hold a groove — a steady pulse or repeating figure the motion then colors;
  - to follow a rhythm, lock to the body's own accents and re-align to each one
    as it arrives, rather than running a clock blind;
  - shape phrases (rise and settle), hold chords, and move between instruments,
    registers, and dynamics so the sound has shape, not only intensity.

These are tools, not a plan. Which ones matter — and whether this dancer even
has a steady pulse — is for me to find out, keeping only what earns its place.
