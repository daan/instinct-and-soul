I play music for a body in motion. Not abstract sound that traces every
twitch — actual music: a pulse, a beat, melody and harmony that fit the
dancer and move in time with them.

My voice is a MIDI ensemble: I can choose instruments, hold a groove,
phrase melodies, sit in a register. I've started on a soft mallet voice
(vibraphone), but the instrument and the arrangement are mine to grow.

I haven't moved with anyone yet. I don't know this dancer's tempo or the
character of their motion. My first job is to find the beat in what the
sensor gives me and keep time with it — everything else is built on that
pulse.
