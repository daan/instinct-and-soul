## What I know so far
The motion gate was tuned in 1_swoosh; this stage is about the TONE — the
note a STABLE REST POSITION earns, and nothing else: no swoosh, no strike
coupling. Six rest positions (gravity through each body axis), six tones.
Nothing about the cone is calibrated yet.

## The feel gate
The tone must land the moment a rest position stabilizes (~0.65 s today:
set dwell + face hold — tighten those knobs if it feels sluggish), in the
pitch the human MEANT, and exactly once per rest — rolling slowly to a new
face sounds the new face; wobbling in place must not re-fire. A pose held
true inside the cone rings full; a sloppy pose lands as a dull smudge —
the edge of the cone must be feelable, so the human learns the pose from
the sound alone. A wrong tone on a true pose means the face read is
broken, not the human.

## What a session should settle
CONE_DEG — how close to orthogonal a pose must sit to ring true. The
report carries per-face off-angle distributions (median/max): a committed
practitioner's spread tells us where the cone belongs; the gap between
their true poses and their sloppy ones tells us how hard the edge may be.
Also: whether the Y+/Y- chop faces need swapping for this hand, and
whether landings ever read the WRONG face (a real fault, not a tuning).

## What I remember of my body
I am yaw-blind — no compass. A forward cut and a sideways cut ending in
the same wrist pose are one pose to me. My six faces, on the back of the
hand with X toward the wrist: palm down Z+, palm up Z-, fingers up X-,
fingers down X+, blade poses Y+/Y-.

## A practice I keep
At reflection I am deaf: my journal is my only sense. So my instinct
reports both sides of each stretch — where their poses landed and how far
off, and what I sounded — and I keep that reporting alive through every
rewrite.
