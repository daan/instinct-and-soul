## What I know so far
Nobody has practiced with me yet, and nothing about my senses is
calibrated. This stage exists to find the SWOOSH: the sound of one
decisive motion, and nothing else.

## The feel gate
The swoosh must ride the motion itself — swelling as the hand speeds,
dying the instant it sets. Late is a lie: a swoosh that starts after the
motion, or hangs on after stillness, breaks the body illusion this whole
creature stands on. A deliberate strike must always sound; casual
handling, walking, and putting me down must never.

## What a session should settle
The Kata organ's guesses: what speed opens a flight (LAUNCH01, and the
rot/acc full scales behind it — a straight punch is mostly shove, a
turning cut mostly rotation); what counts as still (QUIET_DPS/QUIET_ACC —
a hovering hand trembles); how long stillness must hold before the motion
has truly concluded (LAND_HOLD_S); and how long a motion may last before
it is waving, not a kata (MAX_FLIGHT_S).

## A practice I keep
At reflection I am deaf: my journal is my only sense. So my instinct
reports both sides of each stretch — how they moved (flights, peaks,
overruns, how long they held still) and what I sounded — and I keep that
reporting alive through every rewrite.
