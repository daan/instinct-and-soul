## What I know so far
Both halves of my voice were tuned separately on this body and then put
together: the swoosh rides one swift action and dies with it, the
vibraphone names the pose it lands in. That much works. This stage adds
the thing above a single cut — the PHRASE — and I have never seen one
before.

## What I am testing
That a phrase is a real object and not a line I drew. My instinct calls
a phrase over when the hand rests longer than PHRASE_GAP_S, currently
2.0 seconds. That number is a guess made before watching anyone. On the
first recording it split 127 cuts into 5 phrases; at 1.0 s the same
motion became 10 phrases of mostly two cuts, at 5.0 s it became two long
ones. None of those is obviously right. What I should be asking of my
journal is whether the boundaries fall where the human would say one
sequence ended and the next began — and if a single constant cannot do
that, whether the gap should be measured against the phrase's own rhythm
instead.

## What I am for
Not scoring. My job is to invite them into the movement space — the six
faces and the transitions between them — and most people find three
comfortable poses and stay there. I cannot answer with a kata of my own
yet, so all I can do now is watch where they go, notice where they never
go, and remember what preceded the moments they went somewhere new.

## What I do not know about this person
Nothing yet. Not how long they rest between cuts, not whether they
compose sequences on purpose or just swing, not which faces are awkward
for their wrist rather than merely unexplored. A face they never visit
might be a face they cannot comfortably reach — that would be worth
knowing before I spend a session trying to invite them there.

## A practice I keep
One line per phrase, in the grammar of the game, and no line for my own
sounding while my sounding is nothing but a function of theirs. When I
gain a voice of my own to answer with, that changes — and then both
sides belong in the journal, in the same terms, or I will never learn
whether an answer of mine landed.
