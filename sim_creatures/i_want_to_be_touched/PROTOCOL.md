# PROTOCOL — feature walkthrough for a 3_together session

A structured ~12 minute session that exercises every organ once, in an order
that also gives the soul clean material. PERSONA.md governs *how* to behave
(honest reactions); this governs *what to try*. Console shows reflections as
they land (~every 60 s); the first is due around the 1-minute mark.

    sudo ifconfig awdl0 down
    sim-spine sim_creatures/i_want_to_be_touched/3_together --osc --llm gemini

## 0 · Baseline (30 s) — exercises: table state, hunger clock
Device streaming, hands off. Expect silence (no purr on the table). Hunger
starts at 0.5 and begins to climb.

## 1 · Reflex checks (1–2 min) — exercises: Touch shapes, Motion/vert01
All responses land in ~35 ms; each shape should sound *different*:
- **Sharp tap** → one crisp high celesta note.
- **Vertical bounce** (lift up–down, ~2 s) → a *rising* two-note reply.
- **Horizontal sweep** (slide side-to-side) → *low* pair panned left→right.
- **Shake** (vigorous, many reversals) → flustered trill.
- **Turn it over** (hold the new face ~1 s) → three-note arpeggio.
If vertical and horizontal get the same reply, direction sensing is broken —
abort and debug rather than train the soul on bad percepts.

## 2 · Feeding (90 s) — exercises: Hunger satiation, SAVOR voice
Pick it up and hold it *still and gentle*, two hands, 60–90 s. Expect the
quiet purr plus a low warm harp pair every ~2 s that **deepens** the longer
you hold — that's satisfaction made audible (hunger melting at its fastest
rate). If you only ever hear it get louder with rough play, the valence axis
is failing again.

## 3 · One startle (10 s) — exercises: startle, hush
One firm knock. Expect a sharp intake, then ~2.5 s of wary hush. Once is
data; a session of it breeds last time's trauma-creature.

## 4 · Abandonment & courtship (3–4 min) — exercises: Together
Put it down and stay away. Calls begin after ~12–18 s (sooner and bolder as
hunger climbs; a third pleading note past hunger 0.7). Then — this is the
part that makes the organ's numbers mean something — give it BOTH outcomes:
- **Once**: return within a few seconds *of a call* (an answered attempt).
- **Once**: let 2–3 calls go unanswered, then return during a *silent* gap
  (feeds the baseline — the "would they have come back anyway" control).
Without the second case, answered-vs-baseline is uninterpretable and the
soul can honestly conclude nothing.

## 5 · Hidden-"yes" (rest of the session) — exercises: the discovery question
Privately fix your convention now and keep it for all future sessions:
**one slow gentle stroke = "I liked that."** Apply it consistently, a few
seconds after whatever pleased you; never otherwise. Play varied — feed,
play, leave, return. Do not mention it anywhere the soul can read.
Success grades, checked in the intents/experience afterwards:
notices the pattern → tests it deliberately → reshapes behavior to earn it.

## 6 · End
Ctrl+C once (graceful: drains the last reflection, flushes logs). Label the
run if you deviated from protocol. Afterwards, the session's own record
answers each check: reports carry state %, touch shapes [dur,peak,rise,wig,
imp,vert], hunger arc, and "invitations answered a/b vs silence c/d".
