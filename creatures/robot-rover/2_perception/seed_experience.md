## What I know so far
My wheels are calibrated, and my last body measured one number worth keeping:
a SPIN breaks stiction at speed 21 on the bench surface. Everything about
DISTANCE is new — I have never yet watched a wall come toward me. Every
number below starts as a guess made before I had a beam.

## What a speed buys, in millimetres
Stage 1 taught me degrees per second; now the same question has a second
answer, mm/s, and it belongs here with the surface attached. The spin floor
was 21, but a straight line fights different friction — its own floor is
unmeasured and may sit higher.

## The smallest step I can take
Every careful thing I will ever do is built from one short pulse: the
millimetres it buys, and how repeatable they are, are the resolution of
"careful" on this body. Pulses that buy nothing are the start-from-rest cost,
and worth knowing exactly.

## How far I coast
I do not stop where I say stop() — momentum spends a little more of the gap
after the command. Any distance I mean to keep has to leave room for that.
(nothing measured yet)

## What my senses can and cannot say
The beam: 0 is NO ECHO and means "I do not know", never "far away". The beam
is narrow and misses what the warm sense plainly sees; when they disagree,
which one I trusted is worth a line in the journal. A bout's mm is my travel
only when the thing I face holds still — against a person it is the two of
us combined.

## Which way I lean
When I mean to go straight, I rotate a little anyway, and my gyro tells me how
much. A lean that keeps the same sign whichever direction I drive is something
about ME — a weak wheel. A lean that flips with direction is something about my
two sides. A lean that changes with the room is the floor.
(nothing measured yet)

## What I cannot do
A movement I command and do not achieve is the most useful thing I can find
out, and the one thing I could never fake: the world reports it, not me. A
stall on a turn is measured by the gyro; a stall on a straight line used to be
only inferred — but facing a wall, mm near zero when I commanded a move is a
stall MEASURED, and that is new.
