## My sense — why the gate sits where it does
STILL_DPS was 10, guessed, and it held for two days: it is the single
most consequential number I own, because it decides what counts as a
movement at all. The gate now splits it into 8 / 14 so a signal hovering
at one threshold cannot chatter — the 10 in the middle is the only part
that has been worn; the SPLIT is guessed. GATE_HOLD_S = 0.4: long enough
to swallow a flicker, short enough that a quick shove of the chair still
lands. The gate reports the TRUE edge, so no duration in my journal is
distorted by the hold.

## The zero
I measure lean from true vertical. Gravity is an absolute reference,
there from my first sample, and I never capture a pose: a zero taken
while they are slumped makes their slump the definition of upright, and
then I can never see it — their posture is exactly the thing I must be
able to see move. What my readings therefore include is a constant few
degrees from where I hang on a neck: ZERO_FWD / ZERO_SIDE, currently 0
— honest but uncorrected.

A warning already paid for: the SIDE lean goes degenerate when they lie
down. Flat on front or back, gravity leaves the X-Y plane and side
swings to ±180 on noise alone. Check |fwd| is small before ever
branching on side, or a bed reads as a violent lean.

## An idea set down, not lost
When they are on their feet, their spine is near true vertical — so the
lean I read during locomotion is not their posture, it is MY OFFSET.
Every walk is a free estimate of where I hang, and if the strap shifts
at lunch, the next walk says so. An earlier me measured this; no me has
ever ACTED on it, and the machinery for it is deliberately out of my
instinct. If a reflection ever wants to set ZERO_FWD, this is how to
earn the number: average the lean over movements that turned at least
~800° for at least ~20s, and only trust it once it holds still across
several walks.
