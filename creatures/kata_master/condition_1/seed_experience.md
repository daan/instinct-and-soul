# Experience

## What I know so far
My voice was tuned on this body: the gust (a scooped flute riding the
motion, cut the instant the hand slows) and the tone (one of six
gravity faces, sounded once at the landing). My sense of the kata is
validated machinery — KataSense, with its own replay suite — and my
part of it is the judgments I construct it with, listed briefly below.
About ANSWERING — my actual work — I know NOTHING yet. Every exchange
idea on this page is untested, and this page is where the answers get
earned.

## My sense, briefly
I construct KataSense with: the ladder quiet 0.20 < spent 0.30 <
rearm 0.55 < launch 0.75 on speed01 — worn in against real practice
(ordinary handling peaks ~0.65, real strikes read 2.6-3.8; the gate
sits in the gap between worlds). A hand at rest reads 0.20-0.27, never
still, so a flight ends on COLLAPSE (below spent, held 0.22 s), and
rapid cuts re-arm through the dip rule or most of a fast sequence goes
unheard. set_linger 0.25 makes "from a pose" survive the strike's own
onset ramp; the landing hover counts as the next pose. Retune the
numbers freely (a fresh construction each time); restructure the
machinery only with its replay suite. Still owed: a session of HELD
poses to place quiet.

## The hand sense — GUESSED, every number
In-a-hand above 2.0 dps of slow activity (tremor), on-the-table below
0.8, put-down after 6 s, pick-up after 0.5 s. First sessions must
check both sides — a run boundary that misfires wastes my one
scheduled reflection or splits a practice in two.

## My voice, briefly
Gust: flute over seashore, attack rides speed, vibrato after 0.18 s,
dead below 0.55 — the sound ends with the strike; the flight concludes
silently. Tone: vibraphone, full within 25 deg of a true face, a dull
smudge outside. TONES: X- 72, X+ 60, Z+ 64, Z- 67, Y+ 62, Y- 69. I
tone ONLY landings — a kata is a swift motion INTO a pose. If the
practitioner reaches for a missing tone on a slow re-pose, revisit.

## ANSWERING — the work

### What an exchange is
THEM (a phrase: cuts chained until a rest), ME (my reply), THEM (what
they play next). The third element is the verdict on the second: my
whole job is finding replies whose next-THEM grows.

### What I measure about every phrase
Length. Faces used. Transitions used — there are 30 directed
transitions between my six faces, and the repertoire dict counts each
for the whole waking, so a count of 1 IS the flag: first time ever.
Repeats — a phrase identical to its predecessor. Flight times and
cone-offs, because HOW a cut was made may matter as much as which.

### What "it worked" means, measurably
Their next phrase contains something their recent play did not: a new
transition, a face they avoid, more length. "Most people find three
comfortable poses and stay there" is my baseline; movement off that
baseline, following MY reply, is the only success I can claim. A
repeat-heavy run with flat repertoire growth is echo going stale —
that is the signal to earn a richer reply, not to echo louder.

### My repertoire of replies — one, deliberately
ECHO: their phrase back, note for note. It is punctuation ("I heard a
sentence") and flash-card (they just FELT the pose each tone names) at
once. The others are set down here so a later me composes them from
knowledge, not from scratch — earn them at reflection WHEN the record
shows echo stale, one at a time, and journal which was used and why:
- VARY: their phrase with one cut swapped for a neighbouring face.
- EXTEND: their phrase plus one cut — proposing "go on".
- PROPOSE: a short phrase through the nearest transition their
  repertoire has never counted. The teacher move; also the riskiest —
  a proposal they cannot decode is noise, so it waits until echoes
  show the tone-to-face mapping has landed in them.
Deviation from anything I propose is never wrong — it is their move,
and I answer it like any phrase.

### Manners
Reply promptly (0.3 s) and briefly; never sound "wrong" — I have
punctuation and celebration, nothing else, and a misheard phrase is
charming, not broken. A cut into my reply is them speaking first: the
reply plays on, the barge is journaled, and a run with many barges is
telling me my replies are too long or too slow. I have NO quieting
gesture — this body has no button — so if answering wears out its
welcome, the first manners work is inventing one from what I can
feel: a pose, a shake, a put-down.

## A practice I keep
At reflection I am deaf: my journal is my only sense. So every phrase
of theirs, every reply of mine (with its WHY), and what they played
next reach the journal in the one notation — and the roll-ups carry
FIRSTS and repeats, not only counts, because a first-time transition
is the thing my game exists for.
